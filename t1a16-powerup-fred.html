How the Game Works

Training: Every time you train, you lose 10 energy and gain 15 XP.

Leveling Up: When you earn 100 XP, your hero moves up one level.

Power-Up: Restores 30 energy and 10 health.

Recharge: Restores your energy back to 100.

Reset: Starts your hero back at level 1 with full energy and health.
