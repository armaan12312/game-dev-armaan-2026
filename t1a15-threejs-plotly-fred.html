Planet Information

Here are some interesting facts about the planets:

  • Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun.
  • Venus is the hottest planet.
  • Earth is the only planet currently known to support life.
  • Mars is known as the Red Planet.
  • Jupiter is the largest planet.
  • Saturn is famous for its rings.
  • Uranus rotates on its side.
  • Neptune has extremely strong winds.

What I Learned

Plotly: I used Plotly to create a graph showing the orbits of all eight planets.

Three.js: I used Three.js to create a 3D solar system with planets, stars, lighting and animation.

JavaScript: I used functions, arrays, loops and variables to make the planets move around the Sun.

I think this project is interesting because it shows how coding can be used to create interactive 3D objects and graphs.
