My Number Sorting Program

Welcome to my sorting program! This page creates random numbers and lets you sort them from smallest to largest.

Step 1: Click Set Random to create some random numbers.



Step 2: Click Show to see the numbers you generated.



Step 3: Click Sort to put the numbers in order from smallest to largest.



Step 4: Check your results below.

Your numbers will show up here.



Why is sorting useful?

Sorting helps organize numbers so they are easier to read and compare. This program uses a bubble sort to move numbers into the correct order.
